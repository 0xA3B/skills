# Controlled language

Use these mechanical limits only when the user or project requires strict controlled language or
translation-oriented writing. The semantic-preservation rule in `SKILL.md` remains authoritative:
leave a sentence outside these limits when enforcing them would obscure behavior or a relationship
between facts.

## Preferences

- Use a maximum of about 20 words in an instruction sentence and about 25 in a descriptive sentence.
- Avoid the "-ing" form of a verb. A technical name that ends in "-ing" is fine, for example "a
  string" or "load balancing".
- Use no more than three consecutive nouns.
- Use the simple present, simple past, and simple future tenses.
- Use a vertical list when there are more than two conditions, steps, or items.
- Use a maximum of six sentences in a descriptive paragraph.
- Avoid contractions.

## Acceptance

You do not have the licensed ASD-STE100 dictionary, so do not claim that a word or artifact conforms
to ASD-STE100. If the project provides a controlled-language checker, use that checker as the final
acceptance gate.
